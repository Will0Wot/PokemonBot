# Pokémon Adventure Discord Bot

**A text-based Pokémon adventure game played entirely through Discord commands.**

Train your Pokémon, battle wild and trainer-controlled opponents, and explore a vibrant world where every area offers unique encounters and challenges—all within Discord!

---

## Project Overview

The Pokémon Adventure Discord Bot is designed for beginner-friendly gameplay that captures the spirit of classic Pokémon games while leveraging the power of Discord for multiplayer interaction. The core features include:

- **Real-Time Battles:** Engage in animated battles against wild Pokémon and other trainers.
- **Dynamic Encounters:** Explore various regions (e.g., grass patches, Pokémon towns) to discover and catch a wide variety of Pokémon.
- **Discord-Driven Gameplay:** Use simple Discord commands to start your adventure, manage your party, battle, and more.

---

## Key Features

### Core Gameplay Mechanics
- **Battle System:**
  - Real-time battles with visual feedback.
  - Engage in battles with both wild Pokémon and other Discord users.
  
- **Catching & Leveling Up:**
  - Catch Pokémon during your adventures.
  - Earn experience (XP) from battles to level up your Pokémon.
  - Track key stats such as HP, Attack, Defense, etc.

- **Evolution:**
  - Pokémon evolve when they reach specific levels.
  - Future updates may include evolution via items or trades.

### Inventory & Storage
- **Pokémon Party:**
  - Manage an active team of up to 6 Pokémon.
  - Easily swap Pokémon through a dedicated in-game menu.
  
- **Pokémon PC (Storage System):**
  - Automatically store additional Pokémon in your PC if your party is full.
  - Access and manage your Pokémon collection anytime.

- **Items & Economy:**
  - Purchase and use items like Poké Balls, Evolution Stones, and Potions.
  - Start your journey with initial items provided by Professor Oak.

### Adventure & Exploration
- **Adventure Mode:**
  - Trigger adventures using commands (e.g., `!adventure`).
  - Explore different wild areas where random encounters occur.
  
- **Pokémon Towns & Achievements:**
  - Travel from town to town, each offering unique encounters.
  - Unlock new Pokémon and content as you achieve milestones.

### Discord Integration
- **Command-Based Gameplay:**
  - Use commands such as `!start`, `!battle`, `!catch`, `!party`, and `!adventure` to control your journey.
  - Seamless integration with Discord for multiplayer interactions and battle requests.
